Local SEO for Wimbledon businesses

Local SEO is a list of specific jobs: making sure Google understands what you do and where, building pages that answer the searches people really make, keeping your Google Business Profile complete and active, and fixing the technical problems that stop pages being found.

Wimbledon Village, the town centre, South Wimbledon and Raynes Park are searched for by name, and so are the neighbouring parts of Merton. If you cover several, your site should say so plainly, on pages written for those searches.

I do not promise a ranking position, and nobody honest can. What I can show you is the work and the numbers, in your own Search Console: which searches you appear for, how many people click, and how many enquiries arrive.

What local SEO includes

Keyword research

On the searches your customers actually use, from “electrician Wimbledon” to “end of tenancy cleaning Raynes Park”.

Service and area pages

Written for those searches, one page per service or area worth winning, not one page with a list of place names.

Google Business Profile

Set up properly, with the right categories and service areas, then kept active with posts and review management.

A technical pass every month

Page speed, indexing, broken links and structured data checked and fixed.

A plain-English report

Clicks, enquiries and what is changing next, every month, without needing a call to explain it.

How the monthly work runs

Each month I review your Search Console and Bing data, publish one new page or rewrite an existing one where the data says it will move faster, run the technical checks and send the report. If a month passes without that work, that month is free.
