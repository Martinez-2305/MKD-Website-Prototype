The problem

Every order taken by phone
is an order you had to be free for

Takeaways, salons, clinics and shops all hit the same ceiling. Orders and bookings arrive when somebody is available to answer, and stop when they are not. The busiest hour of the day is the hour nobody can get through.

A website that takes the order does not clock off. It takes the booking at eleven at night, holds the slot, sends the confirmation and takes the payment, without anyone picking up.

It also stops the leak that nobody measures: the customer who rang, got the engaged tone, and rang somebody else instead.
