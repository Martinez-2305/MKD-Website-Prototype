What someone checks
in the thirty seconds before calling

They are not reading your site. They are scanning it for three things, and if they cannot find them quickly they go back to the search results.

01

Do you cover me?

The single most common reason a visitor leaves. If your towns are not stated plainly, and there is not a page for the one they live in, they assume the answer is no and move on.

02

Do you do this exact job?

“Building services” tells them nothing. A page that names the job they have — the extension, the consumer unit, the flat roof — tells them they are in the right place, and it is the page that ranks for it.

03

Are you the real thing?

Photographs of your own finished work, your accreditations, your insurance, and reviews with names on them. Stock photos of somebody else’s kitchen do the opposite of what they are there for.
