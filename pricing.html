What I promise, and what I don’t

I don’t promise rankings. Not number one, not page one by a given date. Google’s ranking is affected by your competitors, your industry, your reviews, your area and algorithm changes that nobody outside Google sees coming. Anyone quoting you a guaranteed position is guessing, and you should walk away from them.

What I do promise is the work. Every month you get the data review, the new page, the technical pass and the report. If a month passes without them, that month is free. No arguing, no invoice.

That’s the difference between this and an agency that takes your £199 and goes quiet. You’ll be able to see exactly what was done, every month, and watch the click numbers move.

Questions

Why are your prices on the website?

Because you’re going to ask anyway, and making you sit through a call first wastes your time and mine. If the number doesn’t work for you, you’ve lost thirty seconds instead of an hour.

Which package should I pick?

Starter if you need somewhere credible to send people. Growth if you want the phone to ring — it adds the search-targeted landing pages that do the actual ranking. Bookings & Ordering if the website is the sales channel and you need to take orders, bookings or payments on it.

How long does it take?

Two to three weeks on Starter, three to four on Growth, four to six on Bookings & Ordering. Your website will be live and fully indexed within 30 days of receiving content, or the next monthly payment is waived.

Do I have to write the copy?

No. Copywriting is included in every package. One 30-minute call is all I need — you talk, I write. Copywriting covers the pages listed in your package, drafted from that one content call, with revisions included in your package’s rounds. Rewrites beyond that are billed hourly.

Do I need the SEO Retainer?

It is available with Growth and Bookings & Ordering, and not available on Starter. The build gives you a site that can rank. The retainer is what makes it rank. Minimum 6 months on Growth, 12 months on Bookings & Ordering, rolling monthly after that with 30 days’ notice.

Who owns the site and the domain?

Your domain is registered in your name. You own it, and you keep it if you leave. Your site files are yours on request at any time while the Care Plan is active, and Search Console ownership transfers to you at handover.

What does the Care Plan cost after the free period?

£20 a month or £200 a year. It is free for the first 12 months with Growth and Bookings & Ordering, and required for the first 6 months with Starter.

Can you build me an online shop?

Bookings & Ordering covers taking orders, bookings and payments. If you are selling physical products — stock levels, size and colour variants, shipping bands, returns — that is a different build, quoted individually, from £3,500.

Do you work outside Surrey?

Yes. Most of my work is done remotely and I have clients across the UK. If you’d rather meet, and you’re within reach, I’m happy to.

Is VAT included?

Prices exclude VAT where applicable. Your domain is added to the invoice at cost, usually £12–15 a year. On Bookings & Ordering, Stripe fees and the merchant account are yours.
